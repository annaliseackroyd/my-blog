Week Seven


Weekly Reflection


What went well this sprint?

This week I had a lot more motivation as well as felt more urgency as the deadline was just around the corner. I was able to get a lot more done than previous weeks and although I didn’t get everything done I was still happy that I was able to get a decent amount done. Other than the urgency of the deadline I think the key contributing factor was that past weekend. It’s nothing relating to the assignment but it felt very much a like reset and stress free weekend. Over the weekend I spent time with my family for the first time in two months, I celebrated my birthday with friends, and I saw my favourite girl-group live in concert. With all of this positivity and fun it felt like a total reset and I was able to get back into this assignment after the weekend with a much more positive mindset. Something this week I was proud of was the final presentation. Although I didn’t think it was necessarily my best one as I did forget what to say a few times, the positive feedback, especially from Simon made me feel quite proud of my efforts and more confident about it.


What did not go well this sprint?

What didn’t go so well this week was that I didn’t even make a start on the coded app. Unfortunately since I slacked off so much in previous weeks I essentially sabotaged myself and there was no way to get everything done unless I wanted to pull all-nighters. Something else was that I’m just feeling pretty exhausted. It’s the end of the term and I’ve been working on this for nearly eight weeks so understandably so I’m just very tired. I had long reached the point where I just want it to be over so I can relax and not have to stress about it.


What did you learn?

It wasn’t so much something I learned, but more of a reminder to not leave everything to the last minute. Thanks to all the days where I either did nothing or only put in an hour or two I essentially made it impossible to be able to fully finish this assignment. Despite the large workload and deadlines I tend to have a natural “she’ll be right” attitude towards it and in this case it didn’t quite work out. Unfortunately this is exactly what happened last sprint and although I have learned a lot, having the same problem doesn’t help my case. Despite that in the final week I was able to pick back up my motivation and I consistently spent a lot more hours working on this assignment. Being able to pick myself back up really reminded me that I should spend my free time more meaningfully so I’m able to get back into working hard, as I did after that fun weekend.


What will you do next sprint, with this lesson in mind?

There’s a lot of lessons I want to take into next term. The first being as I mentioned earlier, not leaving everything until the last week. As I did in the last two weeks I want to continue to put in a consistent effort daily and stick to routine. My routine slipped up many times throughout the eight weeks and there was even a week where I did next to nothing. Other than routine this was also because of mental health reasons. My motivation may have suggested that but I was able to overcome my struggles within those last two weeks. How I was feeling had a huge impact on this assignment so my goal next term is to really look after myself, maintain healthy routines, and make sure I have a proper work-life balance. I plan to use the upcoming break to properly rest so I can put a lot more into the next assignment.
