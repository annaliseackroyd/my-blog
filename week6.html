Week Six


Weekly Reflection


What went well this sprint?

This sprint I was able to stay a lot more consistent than last week and I got a lot done! I did have a slight slip up on Wednesday but other than that I’m very happy with my efforts this week. The two main things I got done was my lo-fi prototype and my WIP presentation. Thanks to my laziness the week before I realised I needed to lock in or I wouldn’t have anything new to share in the WIP presentation. Not wanting to embarrass myself was what helped me to work harder so I could actually have something to show.


What did not go well this sprint?

Something that didn’t go so well was that I had too much to do and I didn’t know what to focus on. I created a checklist to track everything that needed completing but I was really stuck on what to prioritise and kept jumping from one task to another. In the long run this is completely fine but at the time I think it would have been more efficient to focus on one task at a time. This way I get a sense of satisfaction upon completion. Something else was that I had conflicted feelings on my WIP presentation. I’m happy enough with my slides but I wasn’t so happy with my delivery. I don’t want to blame the other sessions for running over but I ended up presenting much later than I had anticipated so by the time I reached my turn I had forgotten a lot of what I wanted to say.


What did you learn?

This week I was mostly able to continue the momentum from the weekend and put in a lot more hours. I think having a better sense of routine and work / life balance really helped me to stay consistent. From my reflection last week I was able to use that sense of embarrassment to push myself to work harder. Not only did I want to avoid a repeat of last week but with the WIP presentation at the end of the week I had a sense of urgency to actually get enough done to show for feedback. I said last week that I would try to implement a more structured to do list. I did this at the start of the week but ended up just forgetting about it. Despite that I still knew what I needed to prioritise and I was able to get a decent amount of work done.


What will you do next sprint, with this lesson in mind?

Next week I want to do my best to maintain a consistent work effort and hopefully increase the hours I’m putting in! My family are visiting at the end of the week for my birthday plus I have a concert so it’s a good incentive to work super hard up to that point so I can just relax and have fun. It would also be great to take off some of the pressure so the final week I can focus on coding and the final presentation. I would like to try and take each task one at a time as I did this week.
