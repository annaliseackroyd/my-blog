Week Four


Weekly Reflection


What went well this sprint?

This sprint I was able to stay much more consistent with the amount of work I was putting in. I completed a number of tasks and was able to take a big step forward with this project. Something that went particularly well was that I was able to get on top of my blog and I also delivered my presentation. I was previously a little behind on my blog so I spent a couple hours on Tuesday converting it based on the database lecture and I was able to fill in all of the content I was missing. I also thought my presentation went relatively well. I don’t think it was my best presentation but I’m definitely improving on my speaking and I already knew what I had missed before I received feedback about it.


What did not go well this sprint?

Something that didn’t go so well this sprint was the final push before the presentation. As I was presenting on Thursday this time I left it a little late to create the slides. I started the night before but got quickly tired, I decided to take the gamble and wake up early the next day to complete it. I prefer to not do this as I hate the feeling of rushing to complete something with such a harsh deadline ahead of me. Despite this I decided to take the risk and believe I could finish it in time, which I did, however my laptop completely froze for a couple of minutes right on the deadline for submission. It started responding and I got it in with barely one minute to spare.


What did you learn?

Something I learned was that I present better when I take my time to create the slides and then practice them. Although I’m proud of how I spoke and that I was able to get most of the main points across, there were still many things I could have improved on. I would have liked to spend some more time on the competitors and what my solution does better than them. Another improvement could have been the slides themselves. Some slides were a little wordy and some didn’t have enough words. These fault were because I didn’t leave myself enough time to make the slides perfect nor practice them as much as I would have liked.


What will you do next sprint, with this lesson in mind?

Next sprint I have two key things to keep in mind. Firstly is to give myself more time to get things perfect. Because I left it to the last minute I didn’t have enough time to get my presentation as polished as I would have liked. The other main learning point was to stick to routine. The reason I was able to get much more done this week was because I had simple and clear to-do lists but I also was able to follow somewhat of a routine. This routine meant that it was easier for me to put in more work for this assignment.
