Week Seven


Weekly Reflection


What went well this sprint?

This sprint I was able to complete the draft Hi-fi prototype for one key user flow. It might seem like a small feat however considering how slow my progress has been it feels like I’m one step closer to finishing it. The contributing factor that helped me to lock in and get that done was my family’s arrival into Auckland. I wanted to have something to show for the morning when I was waiting for them to arrive and also while having them here I thought it would be beneficial to have them test out my app. I was also happy to get my type here assignment submitted and out of the way. It was only a small assignment but having it out of the back of my mind has taken a lot of stress away.


What did not go well this sprint?

Because I was completely focused on the type here brief I didn’t leave myself much time for the new enterprise brief. Even then I didn’t spend much time on it daily and found myself procrastinating. Something else that impacted me this week was the anticipation of seeing my family on Friday. Although this did motivate me to try and get some work done so I wouldn’t have to worry about it later, all I really wanted was for it to be Friday. Because of this I spent a lot of my time quite mindlessly in hopes that it would make the day go by faster. I’m kind of embarrassed to admit that, but that was the main contributing factor to my slow week.


What did you learn?

I learned that I should try to use my time more wisely. I didn’t take my learnings from the previous sprint and I lost consistency with the amount of work I was putting in and found that I feel back into bad habits. As I mentioned in my type here reflection, my key learning this week is to branch out more when looking for inspiration and not be afraid to experiment with ideas. What helped me overcome my creative slump was ditching Pinterest and actually looking at a magazine for inspiration. This showed me that I don’t always have to pull my inspiration from online and maybe looking else where might spark the best idea.


What will you do next sprint, with this lesson in mind?

Next week my goal is to try get back into a solid work routine. With the deadline at the end of next week I want to try my best to get as much done as possible and submit on time so I can just relax when it’s over. This is more-so for next term but I want to take the lesson of looking for inspiration outside of Pinterest and screens into my next project. Looking out in the world or even looking at physical media and products, you can find all sorts of inspiration and maybe even spark an idea you wouldn’t have gotten from Pinterest alone.
