Week One


Weekly Reflection


What went well this sprint?

Something that went well this sprint was that I was able to brainstorm plenty of potential topics I was interested in for this summative and I narrowed it down to a couple of options. My goal this sprint was to have picked my topic and started brainstorming but unfortunately I was quite indecisive and I’m still tossing up between a couple of ideas. The ideas however are ones I’m feeling excited about which is why I’m so indecisive. I really want to choose the right one considering this is such a long project. Despite not having decided I feel like I’ve made some okay progress.


What did not go well this sprint?

Something that didn’t go well was my indecisiveness and lazy approach to brainstorming. Since it was the first week back from the break I did still feel like I was in holiday mode. I had gotten used to sitting around and doing what I wanted all day so I struggled to get back into routine. I was also out of Auckland during this time so that definitely contributed to it. The reason I didn’t get very far this week was because I really couldn’t make my mind up. Even now I’m stuck between a few topics and I’m not sure which one I want to do. I also noticed that my brainstorming process was a little lazy. I didn’t log many hours this week and it was mostly just class time but I was constantly brainstorming in my head. It probably wasn’t the most effective as it’s not the most rapid option but I did manage to get some interesting ideas out of it.


What did you learn?

A lesson that I learnt was that I should just commit to an idea and stick with it. Anytime I almost locked in an idea I started swaying towards another topic and doubting my decision. At this stage I’m leaning towards one of my first ideas which is nutrition so I really just need to have the confidence to pick an idea. I also need to get back into routine. I learned that being lazy over the break threw my routine completely off and I kind of forgot how to do certain tasks since I was fairly out of practice.


What will you do next sprint, with this lesson in mind?

Next sprint I will have more confidence in my decisions and I want to spend less time feeling indecisive. To improve my process for the next sprint I want to get back into a solid routine and spend more time prioritising this summative. My first step will be to create a daily to-do list and try my best to stick to it. I hope this will give me a clearer direction so less time is spent contemplating and feeling stuck. Something else I want to do going forward is to fully focus on brainstorming rather than doing it while multitasking. Other than in class I didn’t really spend any fully focused time on it and it was something I just did in my head while spending my time doing something else.
