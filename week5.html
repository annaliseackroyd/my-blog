Week Five


Weekly Reflection


What went well this sprint?

This week may have been extremely lazy but my motivation was heavily boosted at the end of the week. My new macbook finally arrived so as silly as it seems I became much more motivated and excited to work. My main goal this week was to create the Lo-Fi prototype for my app. Unfortunately I wasn’t able to come close to this goal. Something good at least was that I made a start on it by completing some sketches of what it may look like. From there I have narrowed down which screens I will develop into digital prototypes. I also finally finished my two moodboards and sent them for approval. With these completed at least I was able to move one small step forward this week.


What did not go well this sprint?

I legit don’t know what happened during this sprint. The week flew by so fast and I’m embarrassed to admit I genuinely almost did nothing. I didn’t intend for it to happen but my mind was elsewhere and I just kept forgetting to lock in. By the end of the week I was able to get a little bit of work done but nowhere near as much as I would have liked. I’m already behind enough as it is so this was another major set-back. This week was a major learning experience for me and I really want to do better from here.


What did you learn?

I learned that I really have to hold myself more accountable for my own schedule and work. My routine severely slipped up at the start of the week which I think contributed to me having such a lazy week. Last sprint I was able to get much more done as I had the pitch presentation and was consistently putting in a decent amount of work everyday. My main goal last week was just to stay consistent this week which completely didn’t happen. Other than because of the public holiday I think the other main reason I slipped up was because there was no end of week deadline. I think this made me hold myself less accountable and it felt like there wasn’t much consequence to being lazy.


What will you do next sprint, with this lesson in mind?

From the embarrassment of this week it’s unlikely I’ll have a repeat of it especially this close to the deadline. Something I’m going to implement next week to support this is to have more structured to-do lists as well as somewhat of a daily routine. Hopefully by having a proper routine it can set me up for good productive habits and I won’t face another repeat of this week. This was very helpful during week 4 and allowed me to get much more done than I had previously. As long as I hold myself more accountable and work hard I know next week I will achieve a lot more and be more productive.
