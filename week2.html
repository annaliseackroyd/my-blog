Week Two


Weekly Reflection


What went well this sprint?

This sprint I was able to gain a little more motivation and direction. Something that went well was that I was able to overcome my research slump. There was a point where I was extremely stuck and not making any progress. It took me days but I finally realised by just simplifying my tasks, I could make it feel a lot easier and I was able to make more progress from that point. I wasn’t able to complete my goals of this sprint. Even though I wrapped up my initial research, I wasn’t able to get anything else done. I intended to have some interviews done or even to put out a survey but due to that slump and my poor time management I wasn’t able to achieve that.


What did not go well this sprint?

Something that didn’t go so well was the research process. I got super stuck on it and there were days when I barely made any progress. The sources I was looking at were also very repetitive so although it was good to compare the information I probably didn’t need to look at quite so many similar sources. Unfortunately, my poor time management had a big part to play too. Everyday I would say to myself I’ll get this done this afternoon, then this evening and then suddenly the day was over. I think the main reason for this was lack of motivation and also not feeling bothered. The task felt tiring so I kept avoiding it all week.


What did you learn?

The main lesson I learnt was to break up my research into some key questions / starting points. I got really stuck on the research phase as my searches were still very broad. This made the whole task seem super daunting and I procrastinated it a lot. When I tried to overcome this slump I realised my problem was that I made the task seem more difficult than it was. I broke up the task into smaller questions and subjects I could research and this instantly made the task so much easier. Something else I learnt is that I shouldn’t just assume and actually ask. When deciding my target audience between students and women with PCOS (or now PMOS) I just assumed I shouldn’t do PMOS due to it being a health condition as well as difficult to conduct primary research. During the WIP I was actually encouraged to look back into it for my target audience as it was quite niche and would be an interesting direction. If I had just asked prior I wouldn’t be stuck deciding whether to continue with students being my target audience or if I backtrack and focus on PMOS instead.


What will you do next sprint, with this lesson in mind?

For the next sprint I will break up the tasks into smaller tasks. As I mentioned the whole researching phase was quite daunting and the only way I was able to overcome this was by breaking it up into smaller tasks. Not only does this make it more achievable but I also got a small sense of accomplishment after completing each task. This keep my motivation level high and I was able to get more done than previously. Although it seems obvious I will make sure to remember this for any more research and for next term too. Something I would also do next time and continuing this assignment is to ask for help when I get stuck. I guess I was a little shy about it but I would have been much better off if I asked for advice or help going in the right direction. It would have saved me a lot of time and help me realise how simple the process actually is. Going forward I need to get over that and just ask.
