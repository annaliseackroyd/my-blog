Week Three


Weekly Reflection


What went well this sprint?

Something that went well was that I was able to come up with a solution idea I was excited about. Ideating off of my original idea about a single serve meal kit I was able to expand on that idea to make it more unique and engaging. I was also able to complete 4 interviews which I got some very good insights from, some of which I was surprised about. I was glad to complete the interviews as I was feeling a little stressed and guilty about leaving it so late. I knew many of my classmates had already completed their interviews so I was pretty stressed about being left further behind.


What did not go well this sprint?

Although it feels like I achieved more for my project this week I was still constantly behind on everything. Honestly I’ve had some personal issues and low motivation over the last couple of days and I keep telling myself I would work on this later that day but I always run out of time. Despite those reasons, the main reason I always ran out of time was due to distractions. I got pretty addicted to replaying Skyrim and what I thought would be a hour long break turned into a 4 hour gaming session! I really wish I was able to complete more this week as it will only increase my workload in future weeks.


What did you learn?

From this sprint I learned that I should try to minimise distractions. Other than for some personal reasons, my biggest downfall were the constant distractions and temptations. I would try to use playing Skyrim as a motivator and for a fun little break but suddenly the entire evening would be gone. I’ve learned that I should either set a timer for breaks or confiscate Skyrim until I have achieved reasonable progress for the day. From my last reflection I mentioned that going forward I would split my tasks up to make them easier to achieve. This went alright at the start of the week and I broke up my daily tasks to be quick and simple but I found that I lost motivation as the week went on. I think that method will still work and I will try it again next week but with a different mindset.


What will you do next sprint, with this lesson in mind?

Next sprint I want to lock in and spend a lot more time on this summative. I plan to actually try and follow my weekly plan more closely and again I will break up the tasks to feel more simpler and achievable. Going forward I will use video games as a reward rather than for what’s supposed to be a quick break. By minimising distractions I think I will be able to get a lot more done next week. I will also try my best to stay consistent throughout the week especially with the proposal presentation coming up. During the WIP presentation I was up late the night before making the presentation from scratch because I had been lazy all week. Despite being proud of my effort I still wish I had started sooner so I could have been less stressed and tired by the end of the week.
